About Me

My journey began in São Paulo, Brazil, where I grew up and attended Colégio Visconde de Porto Seguro. Selected for the highly competitive bilingual German curriculum, I completed both the German Abitur and the Brazilian Ensino Médio diplomas with academic excellence.

At 18, I moved to Switzerland to pursue a B.Sc. in Computer Science at ETH Zürich ↗ (ranked among the Top 3 CS departments worldwide). During my degree, I was selected for an academic exchange at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), where I worked as an AI Engineer at the GenAI Lab & Professorship of Digital Marketing, developing full-stack AI content automation systems.

Beyond engineering, I actively shape university tech ecosystems: as Board Member & Head of Marketing at ETH Blockchain Club (leading partnerships with the Ethereum Foundation, Solana Foundation, ChainSecurity, and Dialectic); Board Member of Claude Builder Club Zürich (collaborating with Anthropic to drive adoption of Claude tools and agentic workflows); and Co-Founder & Board Member of BRASA Zurich (Brazilian Student Association).

Outside of computer science and academia, I am deeply passionate about music and sports. I have been playing the piano for over 15 years, with a focus on Classical music and Jazz, and I am a big football fan and die-hard supporter of Corinthians.
